Put this on your home screen

iPhone

  1. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. If you see Open as Web App, switch it off.
  4. Tap Add.

Android

  1. Tap the three dots ⋮ in the top corner.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen, then Add.

The cookie icon is called CMS Admin. Tap it and your kitchen opens. About once a month it asks for your email and a code.
